I’m Nico Jan, an online English teacher based in Vancouver.

I create tailored learning experiences that guide students academically, professionally, and personally. With each student, I strive to be the best teammate I can be.

我是 Nico Jan，駐溫哥華的線上英語老師。

我為學生設計量身打造的學習體驗，在學術、職涯、與個人成長三方面提供引導。面對每位學生，我都力求成為最好的學習夥伴。

Progress comes first; results naturally follow.

Together, we set long- and short-term goals and make observable, weekly progress towards them. Using Kanban, we apply project-management practices and treat our growth not only as academic fulfilments but also as personal learning paths.

先重進步，成果自會隨之而來。

我們共同訂定長短期目標，並以每週可觀察的進度穩步前進。透過看板管理，我們導入專案管理做法，把成長視為不僅是學業的完成，更是個人的學習旅程。

We read closely and think out loud together.

We annotate literature and graphic novels in shared spaces, modelling how to notice, question, and connect. Students turn observation into argument, then into clear writing.

我們細讀文本，也把想法說出來。

在共享空間中標註文學與圖像小說，示範如何觀察、提問、與連結。學生把觀察轉化為主張，再化為清楚的寫作。

We connect classwork to the world beyond the page.

We watch short clips and examine reliable websites to build context. Students test claims, compare sources, and apply new ideas to issues that matter to them.

我們把課堂與真實世界對接。

透過短片與可靠網站建立脈絡。學生驗證主張、比較來源，並把新觀念應用到與自己相關的議題。

We build research habits that travel to university.

We use the latest research tools and academic databases to plan, narrow, and verify. Students learn to manage sources, cite responsibly, and write with evidence.

我們養成能帶進大學的研究習慣。

運用最新研究工具與學術資料庫來規劃、聚焦、與查證。學生學會管理來源、正確引用，並以證據寫作。

We create together to make skills stick.

We co-plan and produce essays, presentations, and short talks, then revise with clear goals. Each project builds communication, grammar, writing, and speaking for the next one.

我們一起產出作品，讓能力扎根。

我們共同規劃並完成文章、簡報、與短講，再依明確目標修訂。每個專案都為下一步的溝通、文法、寫作、與口說打底。
