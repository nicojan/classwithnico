# About Nico

## Qualifications

I hold a **BA in English Literature and Language** and a **BEd in Secondary Education** from the University of British Columbia, with an **IB Teaching Certificate for English**.  
I also earned a **CELTA** from the University of Cambridge.

---

## Teaching Approach

Learning with me is built on three principles:

- **Curiosity first**: inspired by Sir Ken Robinson, I want students to ask questions and connect ideas.  
- **Critical engagement**: progress comes from discussing, questioning, and writing together.  
- **Visible growth**: I use project-management methods so students and parents can see weekly progress.

My goal is to equip students with skills that stay useful—whether for exams, university, or a world shaped by new technologies.

---

## Experience and Outcomes

I have taught **online since 2017**, working with students from age 4 to mid-40s. Most are in Canadian and U.S. high schools, including **AP and IB programs**.  

Many of my students have been admitted to leading universities in Canada, the United States, and Asia. Others have made major gains on exams such as the **SAT** and **IELTS** through structured preparation.

---

## What Parents Can Expect

- **1:1 lessons** tailored to each student’s needs.  
- **Weekly, visible progress** tracked in a shared system.  
- **Skills that transfer**: close reading, clear writing, confident speaking, and critical thinking.

I am an **online English teacher based in Vancouver**, chosen by parents for a balance of professional training, years of online experience, and results that last beyond the classroom.
